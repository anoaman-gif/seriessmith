## FLAGS

1. **Electricity generation components do not sum to the total** (`cn_electricity_generation` and its five source rows). Thermal + hydro + nuclear + wind + solar = 9437 for August and 66475 for January to August, against published totals of 9438 and 66476. All six values are reproduced as published; the 1-unit gap is not resolved.
2. **Ownership categories overlap** (`cn_value_added_industry_state_holding`, `_shareholding`, `_foreign_hmt_funded`, `_private`). They are not additive. The source table prefixes three of the four rows with 其中 ("of which") but not the foreign-funded row, so the intended nesting is unclear.
3. **Sub-items overlap their parents** (high-tech manufacturing within manufacturing; primary aluminum within ten non-ferrous metals; saloon cars, SUV and new energy vehicles within motor vehicles, with NEVs overlapping both of the others; smartphones within mobile telephones). Do not sum siblings.
4. **Percentage-point rows have no schema ID convention** (`cn_value_added_industry_yoy_change_mom`, `cn_product_sales_ratio_change_yoy`). The schema defines suffixes for contributions only, so these suffixes were chosen by analogy. July's YoY rate is not stated in this release and was not derived from the 0.7 pp figure.
5. **Count rows use units outside the schema's standard list** (`cn_major_industries_count`, `cn_major_industries_with_value_added_growth`, `cn_products_count`, `cn_products_with_output_growth`): "Industries" and "Products". Names translated, no schema convention.
6. **Export delivery value category** (`cn_export_delivery_value`). Filed under Industry, not Trade: it is an industrial-survey measure defined by NBS as the value of products exported by industrial enterprises, not customs exports. The August YoY is stated as nominal; the January to August YoY basis is not stated.
7. **Revised MoM series** (`cn_value_added_industry`, 2025-08 to 2026-07). NBS revised these via its seasonal adjustment model. Previously published values are not in this source and are not shown. MoM figures are NBS's own seasonally adjusted rates.
8. **Release title and date differ between editions.** The Chinese edition's headline title (2026年8月份规模以上工业增加值增长5.2%) differs from the official English title used as `source_title`; the Chinese edition is dated 2026-09-15 and the English edition page 2026-09-16. `source_date` follows the Chinese source in hand.
9. **Unit and name terminology follows the official English edition** where it diverges from a literal reading: vehicle units are "sets" (Chinese 辆, vehicles); mobile telephone and smartphone units are "sets" (Chinese 台); "Coal" (Chinese 原煤, raw coal); plate glass in "weight cases" (重量箱, no tonnage equivalent given). Tonnes is spelled per the schema; the English edition uses "tons".
10. **Low-precision level** (`cn_output_metal_cutting_machine_tools`): published as 7 and 58 ten-thousand units, integer precision, reproduced as published.
11. **Canonical name differs from source wording** (`cn_value_added_industry`): the source's "Value Added of Industrial Enterprises Above the Designated Size" is stored under the schema's canonical "Value Added of Industry", with the source wording in `notes`.
12. **Material not in the provided text.** The page links a downloadable data table (相关数据表) and the English edition carries chart images; neither was available. The release defines average daily output but publishes no daily figures in the text.
13. **Duplicated page body.** The saved Chinese page contains the release body twice. Every figure was checked against both copies and they agree.
14. **Chemicals, August** (`cn_value_added_chemicals`): prose says unchanged (同比持平), table gives 0.0. Consistent; no conflict.

## Methodology and source note

Produced per `CLAUDE.md` from the Chinese text of the NBS release. Indicator names follow the schema's canonical forms and, beyond those, the terminology of the official English edition of the same release. Every value was extracted digit for digit, then machine-checked: each table row's label and four values, and each prose quote, had to appear in the source text (in both duplicated copies) before the row was accepted. No values were computed, rescaled, estimated or filled from other sources; magnitudes stay in the source's units (ten thousand, hundred million).

NBS methodological notes stated in the release: value added growth rates are real rates after deducting price factors; coverage is industrial enterprises with annual main business revenue of 20 million yuan or above; YoY rates are calculated on enterprise coverage kept as consistent as possible with the current period, so they differ in scope from figures published a year earlier, and cross-regional duplicate output in enterprise groups has been removed; the survey is a monthly full survey with January data exempted; industries follow GB/T 4754-2017; the product sales ratio is sales value divided by gross industrial output value.
