## Industry

Value added of industrial enterprises above designated size rose 5.5 percent year on year in real terms in September, 0.3 percentage points faster than in August, and 0.61 percent month on month. Thirty-one of 41 major industries and 271 of 626 products grew year on year; the release publishes rates for 20 of the 41 industries.

## FLAGS

- **F1. No official English edition used.** Searches of stats.gov.cn's English pages on 2026-10-21 found no English edition of this release. All indicator names are translations, using NBS's standard English terms for GB/T 4754-2017 industries and product names where they exist, and literal translation otherwise. `source_title` is a literal translation of the Chinese title. The schema's worked example cites this release as "Industrial Production Operation in September 2026", published 2026-10-20; the source page shows a different title and a timestamp of 2026/10/19 10:00, so the product uses the source. Affects every row.
- **F2. Units kept in source magnitudes.** Physical and monetary levels are published in 万 (ten thousand) and 亿 (hundred million) units. The schema lists standard forms such as Million Tonnes, Billion Kilowatt-Hours and CNY Billion but does not require rescaling, and hard rule 4 permits conversion only when the schema requires it. Levels therefore carry units such as "Ten Thousand Tonnes" and "CNY Hundred Million" with source digits unchanged. This keeps the values exact but makes them not directly comparable with products expressed in CNY Billion (as in the schema's retail sales example). Maintainer decision needed if rescaling should become a schema requirement. Affects all `cn_output_*`, `cn_crude_oil_processed`, `cn_electricity_generation*`, `cn_export_delivery_value` rows.
- **F3. Industry coverage incomplete.** The release covers 41 major industries but publishes growth rates for only 20 (16 in prose, 20 in the table). The other 21 are not reported and are not in the product. Affects `cn_value_added_*` industry rows.
- **F4. Revised and seasonally adjusted MoM.** Release note 5 revises month-on-month value added growth for 2025-09 through 2026-08 using NBS's seasonal adjustment model. These 12 rows are marked "Revised"; the previously published values are not in the source, so the size of each revision cannot be shown. All MoM figures are NBS's seasonally adjusted rates; nothing was adjusted during conversion. Affects `cn_value_added_industry` MoM rows.
- **F5. Overlapping and nested groups.** The source marks state-holding, share-holding and private enterprises with 其中 ("of which"); the ownership groups overlap and should not be summed. High-tech manufacturing, sedans, new energy vehicles, smartphones and primary aluminum are "of which" subsets. The SUV row sits under motor vehicles without an "of which" marker, so its exact place in the hierarchy is ambiguous.
- **F6. Percentage-point indicators.** The 0.3 percentage-point acceleration in value added growth and the product sales ratio's YoY changes (-0.2 and 0.1 percentage points) are stored as separate indicators in Percentage Points, never in `yoy_pct`. Their names include a change descriptor because they are stand-alone difference indicators; flagged as a naming choice. Affects `cn_value_added_industry_yoy_growth_change`, `cn_product_sales_ratio_yoy_change`.
- **F7. Counts recorded as indicators.** "31 of 41 industries" and "271 of 626 products" growing are stored as count rows, with the denominators in `notes`. Affects `cn_industries_with_value_added_growth`, `cn_products_with_output_growth`.
- **F8. Material referenced but not in the text.** The page links a downloadable data table (相关数据表) and its notes define daily average output (日均产品产量), but no daily average figures or downloadable table contents are in the supplied text. Nothing from them is in the product. The page also references a video that is not part of the text.
- **F9. Identical levels.** Crude steel and solar cells both show 7359 for September (Ten Thousand Tonnes and Ten Thousand Kilowatts respectively) with different YoY rates. Reproduced as published; no evidence of error, but worth checking against the downloadable table.
- **F10. Duplicated source text.** The saved page contains the release body twice (a scraping artifact). Both copies were compared programmatically and are identical line for line.
- **F11. Category choice for export delivery value.** Placed in Industry, since it is an industrial-enterprise indicator from an industrial production release, though it relates to exports. Its growth is nominal, unlike the real value added rates.
- **F12. Value added levels not published.** The table shows "…" for all value added levels, so those rows carry growth rates only with `value` and `unit` empty.
- **F13. Generation subtotals.** Thermal, hydro, nuclear, wind and solar generation sum to 8293 for September and 74915 for January to September, against published totals of 8294 and 74916. The 1-unit gaps look like rounding; values reproduced as published. Check only, not stored in the data.
- **F14. Comparable-scope growth rates.** Per release note 2, YoY rates use a prior-year base restricted to the current enterprise scope, so they will not match rates computed from last year's published levels.

## Methodology and source note

Produced per `CLAUDE.md`: every figure in the release text was extracted, standardized to `schema/product_schema.md`, and traced to a verbatim quote or table cell in `audit.md`. No values were computed, estimated, rescaled or filled from other sources. Release notes state that value added growth rates are real rates after deducting price factors; the scope is industrial enterprises with annual main business revenue of 20 million yuan or more; the survey is a full monthly survey with January data exempt; industries follow GB/T 4754-2017; the product sales ratio is the ratio of sales output value to gross industrial output value; export delivery value covers products exported directly, via foreign trade departments, or produced under processing and compensation trade arrangements. Original title: 2026年9月份规模以上工业增加值增长5.5%. Conversion performed 2026-10-21.
