## FLAGS

- **F1. Table extracted from flattened text** (all table-sourced rows). The release table arrives as one value per line. Columns were assigned from the header order (September absolute value, September YoY, January-September absolute value, January-September YoY); "…" means not published and is left empty. Every value-added and product figure that also appears in the prose (3 sectors, 4 economic types, 16 industries, 8 products, sales ratio, export delivery value) matches the table, which confirms the column assignment. No prose/table inconsistencies were found.
- **F2. No hedged figures.** The release contains no "about" or "approximately" qualifiers.
- **F3. Units kept at source magnitude** (all product rows except robots; `cn_export_delivery_value`). The source uses 10,000 tons, 100 million kWh, 100 million yuan and similar. The schema lists Million Tonnes, Billion Kilowatt-Hours and CNY Billion as standard forms but does not require rescaling, so per hard rule 4 no conversion was performed; units are spelled out as "Ten Thousand Tons", "Hundred Million Kilowatt-Hours", "CNY Hundred Million". "Tons" is kept as published rather than restated as tonnes.
- **F4. Motor vehicle sub-items** (`cn_output_saloon_cars`, `cn_output_suvs`, `cn_output_new_energy_vehicles`). The parent is published in "10,000 sets", the sub-items in "10,000 units". Saloon cars and new energy vehicles carry "Of which"; SUV does not and is treated as a sub-item by position only. The prose confirms new energy vehicles are part of motor vehicles. The sub-items overlap and are not additive.
- **F5. Electricity generation components** (`cn_electricity_generation_thermal`, `_hydro`, `_nuclear`, `_wind`, `_solar`). These follow the total without an "Of which" label and are treated as components by position. They sum to 8293 (September) and 74915 (January-September) against totals of 8294 and 74916, consistent with rounding in the source.
- **F6. Economic types overlap** (`cn_value_added_state_holding`, `_share_holding`, `_foreign_hmt_funded`, `_private`). Three carry "Of which", one does not; the categories are not mutually exclusive and should not be summed.
- **F7. Revised month-on-month rates** (`cn_value_added_industry`, 2025-09 to 2026-08). NBS revised these through its seasonal adjustment model. The previously published values are not in this release, so the size of each revision is unknown. Seasonal adjustment was performed by NBS, not in this conversion.
- **F8. Change in growth rate** (`cn_value_added_industry_yoy_rate_change`). "0.3 percentage points higher than the previous month" is a difference between growth rates and is stored in Percentage Points. The August YoY rate it implies is not stated in this release and was not derived.
- **F9. Partial breakdowns.** The release references 41 major industries but publishes value added for 20; it references 626 products but publishes output for 36. "Average daily output" is defined in the annotations but no figures are given. Nothing was filled in.
- **F10. "(100%)" qualifier** (`cn_output_sulfuric_acid`, `cn_output_caustic_soda`). The release does not define it; the label is kept literally in the indicator name.
- **F11. Change types differ** (`cn_export_delivery_value`, `cn_product_sales_ratio_yoy_change`). Export delivery value growth is nominal, unlike the real value-added rates. Its category is Industry because it measures industrial enterprises' deliveries; Trade is a defensible alternative. The sales ratio change is published in percentage points, so it is stored as its own row rather than in `yoy_pct`.
- **F12. Count units** (`cn_major_industries_with_yoy_growth`, `cn_products_with_yoy_output_growth`). "Number of Industries" and "Number of Products" are not in the schema's standard unit list. The denominators (41, 626) are recorded in `notes`.
- **F13. Comparability and precision.** Per the release, product output YoY rates are calculated on a comparable enterprise coverage, so they cannot be reproduced from last year's published levels. Metal-cutting machine tools (`cn_output_metal_cutting_machine_tools`) are published to integer precision (8 and 67 ten-thousand units), so the published growth rates carry more information than the levels.
- **F14. Category choice for energy products** (`cn_output_coal`, `_coke`, `_crude_oil`, `_natural_gas`, `cn_crude_oil_processing`). The schema's Industry ("output of industrial products") and Energy ("energy production") categories overlap here. Energy was chosen; electricity generation is unambiguously Energy.

## Methodology and source note

Produced under `CLAUDE.md`: the full release was read, every quantitative figure in prose, table and annotations was extracted, standardized to `schema/product_schema.md`, and traced to a source location in `audit.md`. Every table value in `data.csv` was checked programmatically against the parsed source table by row label and column, and every number against the source text. No values were computed, annualized, adjusted, rounded or converted. Where prose and table state the same figure in different magnitudes (for example 118.17 million tons and 11817 ten-thousand tons of rolled steel), the table magnitude is used so the September and cumulative rows share a unit.

Source methodology, as stated in the release: value-added growth rates are real rates after deducting price factors. Coverage is industrial enterprises with annual main business revenue of 20 million yuan or above; because coverage changes yearly, YoY rates are computed on a comparable coverage with cross-regional duplicate counting removed. Data come from a comprehensive monthly survey, with reporting exempted for January. Industries follow GB/T 4754-2017. Month-on-month rates come from a seasonal adjustment model and were revised for September 2025 to August 2026.

The source is in English; no translation was needed. Conversion performed 2026-10-21.
